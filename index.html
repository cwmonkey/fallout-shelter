Introduction

This tool fixes your "experimental" season valt save (Vault4.sav) and allows you to save it as a "normal" vault (Vault1.sav, Vault2.sav or Vault3.sav).

Once a season is over, your experimental vault is deleted and cannot be recovered, so you need to make a backup of Vault4.sav before the season ends.

Fixer

Step 1: Make A Backup

You should always backup your saves before messing with your saves, just in case.

Directory on Android:

Internal storage → Android → data → com.bethsoft.falloutshelter → files

On Steam/PC:

%LocalAppData%\FalloutShelter

Vault1.sav, Vault2.sav and Vault3.sav are "normal" vaults. Vault4.sav is the "experimental" season vault. The spd.dat file contains account information.
